Spotted farms

Structures in Conway’s Game of Life that feed a parked rover forever. They were found by the farmer rovers from LIDAR, part 1.7, logged by a tool that runs the same simulation offline, and checked in isolation before they count. This page updates itself when the tool spots a new one.

    Farms the rovers use

    Each one is shown on its own, looping forever: green cells, the rover as a white square, and a blue ring every time a cell is born under it. Most used first.

    Which structures survive being eaten

    Every common still life and oscillator, with a rover parked on every square on or next to it, in every phase. “Feeds forever” means at least one of those squares keeps delivering food after the structure settles.

    How a farm is counted

    1. A rover parks when it eats on one square several times in a row without moving. Every meal after the first has to be a cell born under it.
    2. A parking session with at least 10 harvests is a candidate. Its structure is everything connected to the live cells within 2 squares of the rover, from a snapshot taken at its 10th harvest. Neighbouring objects the feeding doesn’t need are removed.
    3. The structure is tested alone, with a rover parked on the same square and eating every generation. If it settles into a cycle with at least one meal in it, it’s a farm. Candidates that stop feeding on their own were fed by their surroundings, and are only counted.
    4. Each farm’s spotted count is the number of distinct places it turned up. The same structure at the same spot in one run counts once, however many rovers took turns on it.

    Every 6 hours a scheduled job runs the logger on seeds it hasn’t tried yet and republishes the data, and this page picks up anything new on its own. To search more yourself, run the logger from the site folder. With --serve it also serves this page locally, so you can watch farms appear:

    node tools/farm-log.js --watch --serve 8080
